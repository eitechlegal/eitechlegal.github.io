India DPDP Act compliance, from the legal requirements down to your databases

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is now in force through the DPDP Rules, 2025, with full obligations applying from 13 May 2027. Why act now

The deadline is fixed

The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025 with an 18-month phased rollout. Notice, consent, security safeguards, breach notification, retention and data-subject rights obligations apply from 13 May 2027.

Penalties are significant

The Data Protection Board of India can impose penalties of up to ₹250 crore for failing to take reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach.

Most of the work is technical

Policies alone aren't enough. You need to know what personal data you hold, where it's stored, who can access it, how long you keep it and how you would detect and report a breach.
