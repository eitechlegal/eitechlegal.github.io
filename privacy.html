Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

EiTech Advisory Group ("we", "us", "our") is a company registered in Singapore that operates the eitechlegal.com website. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website and our services, how we use it and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and, where it applies, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Personal data we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, company name and the message you send us.
  • Emails and calls: any details you share when you contact us directly or become a client.
  • Client engagements: business contact details of the people we work with. Where our work involves access to your organisation's systems or records, we handle that data only as instructed by you and under our engagement terms.
  • Website use: this website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as IP addresses for security and service operation.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a consultation.
  • To provide, manage and invoice our services.
  • To meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for marketing unless you have agreed to receive updates from us. You can opt out at any time.

3. Service providers and transfers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run this website and our business, including:

  • GitHub Pages to host this website.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to deliver contact form messages to our email inbox.
  • Our email provider, to send and receive messages.
  • Oracle APEX (oracleapex.com) to host our property document verification tool, for users who choose to sign in and use it.

Some of these providers process data outside Singapore. Where personal data is transferred overseas, we take reasonable steps to make sure it receives a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and client records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting requirements. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

5. How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorised access, use, loss or disclosure, including restricted access, strong authentication and encryption where appropriate.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used;
  • correct personal data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • stop using your personal data, by withdrawing your consent. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer respond to your enquiry or provide our services.

We will respond within a reasonable time, normally within 30 days.

7. Contact our Data Protection Officer

For any question, request or complaint about your personal data, email our Data Protection Officer at contact@eitechlegal.com with the subject line "Personal Data".

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
