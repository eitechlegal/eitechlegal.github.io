About EiTech Advisory Group

EiTech Advisory Group is a boutique advisory firm specializing in data protection, cybersecurity and regulatory compliance for organisations in Singapore and the region. We combine legal training with more than 25 years of hands-on enterprise IT experience, so compliance programmes are grounded in how your systems and data actually work.

About our founder

Our founder is an IT infrastructure leader with more than 25 years of experience across Oracle and multi-platform database administration, storage, data-centre operations and enterprise application development.

The founder holds an LLB and a Master's in Law, a Master's in Software Engineering and an MBA, and is certified in security and privacy:

  • ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor
  • CCISO (Certified Chief Information Security Officer)
  • CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker)
  • CompTIA CySA+
  • Microsoft Azure Security Engineer (AZ-500)
  • Personal data protection certification
