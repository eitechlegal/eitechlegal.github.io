DPDP Act Compliance Services

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies in full from 13 May 2027. We help organisations in India, and Singapore businesses serving Indian customers, find where personal data sits in their systems, close the gaps and prepare before the deadline: readiness assessments, personal data discovery in your databases, security safeguards and breach readiness.
